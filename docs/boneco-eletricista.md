# Boneco da patrulha do eletricista

Arte criada com a ferramenta integrada **imagegen**, usando os bonecos urbanos existentes como referência. Capacete amarelo, uniforme azul com faixas refletivas, botas e cinto de ferramentas; versões masculina e feminina, vistas de frente e costas, com quatro quadros de caminhada.

Arquivos em `src/assets/patrol/avatar/figura/`: fontes `eletricista-poses.png` e `eletricista-walk.png`; publicados `masculino-eletricista-{frente,costas}.webp`, `feminino-eletricista-{frente,costas}.webp` e respectivos `*-costas-walk-4x1.webp`. O app inclui somente os WebP. Reproduzir recortes e publicação com `node scripts/patrol-avatar-sprites.mjs --eletricista`; as figuras usam 384×480 e pés na linha de 95%, como o boneco atual.

`toElectricianPatrolAvatar` aplica o uniforme somente no painel do eletricista. A escolha de sexo e carro é compartilhada com as preferências existentes; o uniforme profissional não substitui o traje urbano do cidadão. Preparação, escolha e marcador Leaflet usam o mesmo renderizador. De carro, mantém-se o veículo escolhido.

Validação: `tools/validate-electrician-patrol-ui.mjs` carrega os WebP reais e confere preparação, escolha feminina, marcador parado e fluxo da patrulha em 390, 1440 e 1920 px. Os screenshots ficam em `.tmp/electrician-patrol-validation/`.

## Prompts utilizados

### Personagens e poses neutras

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 3D character sprite sheet for a municipal electrician GPS patrol app. Reference 1 is the existing male walking game avatar from behind; reference 2 is its matching female style from the front. Create electrician versions in exactly the same polished stylized 3D miniature/chibi style, large heads, detailed fabric, soft upper-left studio lighting, warm medium skin. Outfit: yellow safety hardhat, navy work jacket and trousers with silver reflective bands on shoulders and calves, brown work boots, compact electrician tool belt with pliers at hip. Female has dark ponytail tucked below helmet. No backpack, no text, logos, background or ground shadow. Produce a perfectly regular SIX COLUMN by TWO ROW contact sheet on real transparent background, twelve separate complete figures with generous empty gutters, no touching or cropping. First row is the SAME male electrician in all six cells; second row is the SAME female electrician in all six cells. For EACH ROW: column 1 full body facing straight front standing neutrally; column 2 full body facing straight back standing neutrally; columns 3,4,5,6 four consecutive frames of a walking cycle facing straight BACK (left forward contact, passing, right forward contact, passing). Alternate legs and opposite arms visibly but keep head and torso at consistent heights and camera. All camera directions straight-on orthographic, never side or three-quarter. Keep identical scale, outfit, lighting and helmet across the sheet. Head to soles completely visible in every cell. The figures should occupy about 80% of each cell height with feet on same baseline. Landscape canvas approx 3072 by 1280, regular 6x2 grid, transparent, no drawn grid. Final runtime sprites will be 384x480 each.

### Ciclo de caminhada ajustado

Edit target: the provided transparent electrician character sheet. Keep EXACTLY these two character designs, same yellow hardhats, navy reflective uniforms, tool belts, brown boots, medium skin and female ponytail, same straight back camera, lighting, scale and chibi 3D quality. Replace the layout with FOUR COLUMNS and TWO ROWS (8 characters total). Male row 1, female row 2. This must be a WALK CYCLE, not four repeated right-foot-lifted poses. Column 1: LEFT leg stretched back toward viewer showing LEFT boot sole; RIGHT leg planted forward away from viewer; LEFT arm swings forward away from viewer and RIGHT arm back toward viewer. Column 2: legs pass under body, almost together, left knee bent lifting. Column 3: RIGHT leg stretched back toward viewer showing RIGHT boot sole; LEFT leg planted forward away from viewer; RIGHT arm forward and LEFT arm back. Column 4: reverse passing pose with right knee bent lifting. Columns 1 and 3 MUST show opposite soles; in this straight back camera left leg appears on image left, right leg on image right. Each character walks away from camera in place, never lateral or three-quarter. Opposite arms and legs alternate. Same helmet/torso level and same baseline each frame. Perfectly regular 4x2 grid with all full bodies fully visible, centered inside own cell, generous transparent gutters. Transparent background, no text, ground shadows, props, border or drawn grid. Canvas landscape about 2048x1280.
